# Vsales

**Vsales** is a lightweight inventory and sales management solution designed for small and medium-sized businesses, including retail shops, supermarkets, pharmacies, and other organizations that manage stock and sales.

**Current Version:** 2.0.1

## Key Features

* Manage products and services.
* Register customers and track purchases, balances, and statements.
* Track financial transactions with user-level accountability.
* Manage stock receipts, issues, and transfers between stores.
* Monitor product expiry dates and stock levels.
* Receive low-stock and out-of-stock alerts.
* Generate stock documents such as:

  * Goods Received Notes (GRN)
  * Stock Requisition/Issue Notes
  * Stock Transfer Notes
* Record sales and automatically generate:

  * Quotations
  * Cash Sale Receipts
  * Invoices
* Record customer invoice payments and generate payment receipts.
* Produce operational and financial reports, including:

  * Products and Services
  * Customer Lists
  * Daily Sales
  * Customer Statements
  * Stock Content and Movement
  * Financial Reports
  * Audit Reports

Vsales provides a simple way for businesses to control inventory, monitor sales, manage customer accounts, and maintain visibility over day-to-day stock and financial activities.
